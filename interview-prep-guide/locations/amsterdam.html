When you arrive

Getting to the lobby

Please head to the main WeWork entrance from Stadhouderskade 5. Use the tablets available at the reception desk to register as a Box guest (choose your interviewer as host).

Check-in

Head to the main entrance. Let reception know you're here for Box, then sign in on the tablet and add your interviewer as your host.

Required

Photo ID

Please bring a valid government ID.

NDA

Before your scheduled interview, please ensure that you electronically sign our NDA using Box Sign through the provided link.

All candidates are required to sign in order to begin their interview. If you have any questions regarding the NDA form, please reach out to your Recruiting Coordinator.

Arrival & departure

Your interviewer will meet you at the reception and escort you to the conference room.

Questions?

Got a question we didn't cover? Reach out to your Recruiter and Recruiting Coordinator.
