Getting here

Varso Place underground parking

Address: Chmielna 71, Warszawa — located on levels -1 and -2 in Varso 1 and on level -1 in Varso 2.

  • Monday–Friday 7:00–18:00: 10 PLN/hour
  • Monday–Friday 18:00–7:00: 7 PLN/hour
  • Pay at automatic cash registers or by card/cash at reception in the main lobby
  • Head to the "F" elevators to reach the ground floor, then right to reception

Private car park P-35

  • Head to the main Varso entrance at the Chmielna/Jana Pawła II intersection
  • Use your QR code to access the elevators on the right of the main entrance (left of the reception)
  • The QR code will direct you to Box reception on the 22nd floor

Street parking

City parking spots around the building (pay in parking meter or via Skycash/your bank app — mobi Parking).

When you arrive

Getting to the lobby

Head to the main Varso entrance at the Chmielna/Jana Pawła II intersection. Check in with the Varso Tower ground floor reception; present your ID and let them know you are there for an interview with Box. The receptionist will provide you with a printed QR code and direct you to the appropriate elevators to access the 22nd floor where Box is located.

Check-in

Once you arrive at the 22nd floor, please use the tablets available at the reception desk to register as a Box guest and select the name of your Recruiting Coordinator. You may be asked to sign the NDA again. Your coordinator will meet you in the lobby and help you get started with your interviews.

Required

Photo ID

Please bring a valid government ID.

NDA

Before your scheduled interview, please ensure that you electronically sign our NDA using Box Sign through the provided link.

All candidates are required to sign in order to begin their interview. If you have any questions regarding the NDA form, please reach out to your Recruiting Coordinator.

Arrival & departure

Please keep the printed QR code you received from the Varso Tower ground floor reception with you, as you may need it to exit through the gates on the ground floor.
