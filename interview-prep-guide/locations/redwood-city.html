Getting here

If you are taking public transportation, we are conveniently located next to the Redwood City Caltrain station. If you are driving, please review the instructions below:

1

Parking in the garage

The garage entrance is located between our two Box buildings at the intersection of Middlefield Road & Jefferson Avenue.

Note: While our mailing address is 900 Jefferson, the employee garage is accessed via 900 Middlefield.

The building you'll interview in is 900 Jefferson, which features the large Box logo in the lobby.

Even if the garage sign says "FULL", it is for employee access only, which includes candidates and visitors with authorized access.

At the garage entrance, press the Security button to speak with our Security team. Notify them that you have an interview with Box and they will admit you.

2

Taking the elevator to our lobby

Important: You will still need to use the guest/public elevator to reach the lobby. Only Box employees have access to the other designated elevators.

Press the "G" button once in the public elevator.

3

Arriving to the lobby

Once on the ground floor, exit the building and proceed straight to Box's lobby front doors.

Upon entering, check in with reception and let them know who you are meeting with first.

Upon departing, head through the double doors on the right side of reception to return to the garage elevator.

Checking in

Photo ID

Please bring a valid government-issued ID. You will need it for check-in.

Visitor badge

Your interviewer or host will escort you during your visit. When you are finished, return any visitor badge to reception before you leave the building.

NDA

Before your scheduled interview, please ensure that you electronically sign our NDA using Box Sign through the link provided by your Recruiting Coordinator.

During the interview

Laptop

We offer loaner laptops. If you will need a loaner laptop, please reach out to your Recruiter and Recruiting Coordinator as soon as possible.

Ordering lunch

You will be sent a link to order food from a restaurant of your choice via food delivery app by your coordinator. A Boxer will bring it to you when it's delivered.

Taking a break

Please do not hesitate to ask an interviewer for a restroom break or a refreshment from the kitchen between interviews. We want you at your best!

Questions?

Got a question we didn't cover? Reach out to your Recruiter and Recruiting Coordinator.
