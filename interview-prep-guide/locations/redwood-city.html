Getting here

If you are driving, please review the parking instructions below. If you are taking public transportation, we are conveniently located next to the Redwood City Caltrain station.

1

Parking in the garage

The garage entrance is located between our two Box buildings at the intersection of Middlefield Road & Jefferson Avenue.

Even if the garage sign says "FULL", it is for employee access only, which includes candidates and visitors with authorized access.

At the garage entrance, press the Security button to speak with our Security team. Notify them that you have an interview with Box and they will admit you.

2

Taking the elevator to our lobby

Important: You will need to use the guest/public elevator to reach the lobby. Only Box employees have access to the other designated elevators.

Press the "G" button once in the public elevator.

Once on the ground floor, exit the building and proceed straight to Box's lobby front doors.

3

Checking in at the lobby

Upon entering, check in with reception and let them know who you are meeting with first.

Your interviewer will meet you in the lobby, give you a quick office tour, then help you get started with your interviews.

Checking in

Photo ID

Please bring a valid government ID (license, passport, or other government-issued ID) to verify your identity at check-in.

NDA

Before your scheduled interview, please ensure that you electronically sign our NDA using Box Sign through the link provided by your Recruiting Coordinator.

Laptop

Please bring a laptop if you are completing a technical assessment or presentation. If you need to borrow one, please let your Recruiting partners know.

During the interview

Ordering lunch

You will be sent a link to order food from a restaurant of your choice via food delivery app by your Recruiting Coordinator. A Boxer will bring it to you when it's delivered.

Taking a break

Please do not hesitate to ask an interviewer for a restroom break or a refreshment from the kitchen between interviews. We want you at your best!

Walk-out instructions

After your interviews are complete, either your last interviewer or someone on the recruiting team will meet you in the conference room and escort you back to the ground floor.

Questions?

Got a question we didn't cover? Reach out to your Recruiter and Recruiting Coordinator.
