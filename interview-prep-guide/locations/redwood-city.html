Getting here

Driving & parking

The garage entrance is located between our two Box buildings at the intersection of Middlefield Road & Jefferson Avenue.Note: While our mailing address is 900 Jefferson, the employee garage is accessed via 900 Middlefield.Even if the garage sign says FULL, it is for employee access only — which includes candidates and visitors with authorized access. At the garage entrance, press the Security button to speak with our Security team. Notify them that you have an interview with Box and they will admit you.

Public transportation

We are conveniently located next to the Redwood City Caltrain station for those who prefer to take Caltrain.

When you arrive

Getting to the lobby

Important: You will need to use the guest/public elevator to reach the lobby. Only Box employees have access to the other designated elevators.Press the "G" button once in the public elevator.Once on the ground floor, exit the building and proceed straight to Box's lobby front doors.

Check-in

Upon entering, check in with reception and let them know who you are meeting with first.Your interviewer will meet you in the lobby, give you a quick office tour, then help you get started with your interviews.

Required

Photo ID

Please bring a valid government-issued ID. You will need it for check-in.

NDA

Before your scheduled interview, please ensure that you electronically sign our NDA using Box Sign through the link provided by your Recruiting Coordinator.

All candidates are required to sign in order to begin their interview. If you have any questions regarding the NDA form, please reach out to your Recruiting Coordinator.

Arrival & departure

Your interviewer or host will escort you during your visit. When you are finished, return any visitor badge to reception before you leave the building.

During your interview

Ordering food

You will be sent a link to order food from a restaurant of your choice via food delivery app by your coordinator. A Boxer will bring it to you when it's delivered.

Questions?

Got a question we didn't cover? Reach out to your Recruiter and Recruiting Coordinator.
