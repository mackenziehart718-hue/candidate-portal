Getting here

Parking & public transportation

Taking public transit (CTA/Metra) or rideshare (Uber/Lyft) is recommended. There is no parking garage — we recommend using SpotHero.

  • Washington & Wells (4 min walk) — Pink, Brown, Purple, and Orange lines
  • Clark & Lake (10 min walk) — Pink, Brown, Purple, Orange, Blue, and Green lines
  • Washington (8 min walk) — Blue line
  • Clinton (8 min walk) — Pink and Green lines
  • Wacker & Randolph bus stop (1 min walk)
  • Union Station — approximately 7 minute walk (5 blocks)
  • Ogilvie Station — approximately 5 minute walk (2 blocks)

When you arrive

Getting to the lobby

Building entrance is on Wacker Drive, next to the corner of Wacker and Randolph.

Check-in

Please check in with security at the front desk lobby with your government ID, 15 minutes prior to your interview.

Required

Photo ID

Please bring a valid government ID.

NDA

Before your scheduled interview, please ensure that you electronically sign our NDA using Box Sign through the provided link.

Arrival & departure

Once you arrive to the 7th floor, please use the tablets available at the reception desk to register as a Box guest, choose the host name, sign the NDA (if you did not sign it prior to), and take your photo. The WPS specialist will print out your name tag.
