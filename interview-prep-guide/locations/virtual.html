Virtual Zoom Interview

Where to join

Zoom link located in your Google Calendar invitation.

Preparing for your interview

Before you join

  • Find a quiet, private space with good lighting
  • Confirm the Zoom link is working — we recommend downloading the desktop application
  • Test your camera, microphone, speakers and internet connection ahead of time
  • Close extra tabs or applications to avoid notifications or lag

Joining your interview

You will be automatically placed in a waiting room and will need to be admitted by the first interviewer. If you leave the session at any time, you will need to be admitted again.

AI notetakers

The use of Zoom AI features or any personal recording, transcription, or automated note-taking tools during live interviews is not permitted.

Required

NDA

Before your scheduled interview, please ensure that you electronically sign our NDA using Box Sign through the provided link.

All candidates are required to sign in order to begin their interview. If you have any questions regarding the NDA form, please reach out to your Recruiting Coordinator.
