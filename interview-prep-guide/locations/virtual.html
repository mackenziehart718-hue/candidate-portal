Virtual (Zoom)

Remote interview

💻 Virtual interview

Where to go

No physical address — join via the Zoom link in your calendar invite.

Before you join

Your meeting link

Use the Zoom link in your calendar invite only. Do not share it publicly. Each session may use the same or different links — follow your schedule.

Tech checklist

  • Download Zoom ahead of time (zoom.us/download)
  • Test camera and microphone
  • Use headphones to reduce echo
  • Find a quiet, well-lit space
  • Join 2–3 minutes early — you'll wait in the waiting room

When you arrive

Required

🪪 Photo ID

Government-issued photo ID is required for identity verification if requested by your recruiter. No ID = we may need to reschedule.

📄 NDA

No physical NDA. If required, your recruiter will send a DocuSign link ahead of time.

🚪 Arrival & departure

When your interview block ends, you can leave the Zoom call. Your recruiter will share next steps by email.

Questions?

Got a question we didn't cover? Reach out to your Recruiter and Recruiting Coordinator.
