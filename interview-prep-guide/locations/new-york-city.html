Getting here

Parking

  • No on-site parking. Garages on 36th–37th St (~$25–40).
  • We strongly recommend subway or rideshare.

When you arrive

Lobby instructions

Enter on Broadway. Check in with building security in the lobby, then proceed to 4th floor.

Required

Photo ID

Please bring a valid government-issued ID. You will need it for check-in.

NDA

Before your scheduled interview, please ensure that you electronically sign our NDA using Box Sign through the link provided by your Recruiting Coordinator.

All candidates are required to sign in order to begin their interview. If you have any questions regarding the NDA form, please reach out to your Recruiting Coordinator.

Arrival & departure

Escorted to interview rooms. Sign out at front desk when leaving.

Questions?

Got a question we didn't cover? Reach out to your Recruiter and Recruiting Coordinator.
