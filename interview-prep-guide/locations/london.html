Getting here

Parking

  • Street parking is available on Mallow Street (located behind the building).
  • Paid parking is available at NCP London Finsbury Square.

Public transportation

  • Old Street Station: Northern Line (2 min walk)
  • Liverpool Street Station: Elizabeth Line, Overground, Central, Circle, Hammersmith & City, Metropolitan and National Rail Services (10–15 min walk)
  • Liverpool Street also services lines to Heathrow Airport and Stansted Airport

When you arrive

Getting to the lobby

When you arrive, please sign in at the reception desk on the ground floor of the White Collar Factory and state that you are visiting Box. You will be sent up to the 15th Floor where Box's reception is located.

Check-in

Once on the 15th floor, the Box receptionist will ask you to sign in at Box and complete a visitor non-disclosure agreement, if you haven't already done so.

Required

Photo ID

Please bring a valid government ID.

NDA

Before your scheduled interview, please ensure that you electronically sign our NDA using Box Sign through the provided link.

All candidates are required to sign in order to begin their interview. If you have any questions regarding the NDA form, please reach out to your Recruiting Coordinator.

Arrival & departure

After your last interview, your interviewer will walk you to the elevator.
