Getting here

Parking & public transportation

🚗

Driving & parking

The garage entrance is located between our two Box buildings at the intersection of Middlefield Road & Jefferson Avenue.

Note: While our mailing address is 900 Jefferson, the employee garage is accessed via 900 Middlefield.

Even if the garage sign says FULL, it is for employee access only — which includes candidates and visitors with authorized access.

At the garage entrance, press the Security button to speak with our Security team. Notify them that you have an interview with Box and they will admit you.

🚆

Public transportation

We are conveniently located next to the Redwood City Caltrain station for those who prefer to take Caltrain.

When you arrive

Getting to the lobby & check-in

🛗

Getting to the lobby

Important: You will need to use the guest/public elevator to reach the lobby. Only Box employees have access to the other designated elevators.

Press the "G" button once in the public elevator.

Once on the ground floor, exit the building and proceed straight to Box's lobby front doors.

✅

Check-in

Upon entering, check in with reception and let them know who you are meeting with first.

Your interviewer will meet you in the lobby, give you a quick office tour, then help you get started with your interviews.

Required

Registration

Photo ID

Please bring a valid government-issued ID. You will need it for check-in.

NDA

Before your scheduled interview, please ensure that you electronically sign our NDA using Box Sign through the link provided by your Recruiting Coordinator.

All candidates are required to sign in order to begin their interview. If you have any questions regarding the NDA form, please reach out to your Recruiting Coordinator.

Arrival & departure

Your interviewer or host will escort you during your visit. When you are finished, return any visitor badge to reception before you leave the building.

Day-of

FAQs

Lunch
If your interview loop includes lunch, your Recruiting Coordinator will confirm the details in advance. Please share any dietary restrictions with your coordinator as early as possible.
Technology & what to bring
Bring a valid government ID. A notebook and pen are welcome. Only bring a laptop if your recruiter requested a presentation. Please keep your phone on silent during interviews.
Reasonable accommodation request
Box is committed to providing reasonable accommodations for candidates. If you need an accommodation for your interview, please contact your Recruiting Coordinator as soon as possible so we can support you.
What if I'm running late?
Contact your Recruiting Coordinator immediately. We'll do our best to adjust, but some sessions may need to be rescheduled.

Help

Questions?

Contact your Recruiting Coordinator using the email from your calendar invite or confirmation email.
