Before your interview

How to best prepare

Even 15–20 minutes here goes a long way. Focus on what fits your role.

  1. 1

    Re-read the job description

    Review responsibilities and required skills. Think of 1–2 real examples from your experience for each major area.

  2. 2

    Review your work

    Be ready to walk through a project you're proud of — your role, challenges, and what you learned. Bring samples only if your recruiter requested them.

  3. 3

    Learn about us

    Skim the links below — our careers site, product overview, values, and Aaron's video. Genuine curiosity matters more than memorizing facts.

  4. 4

    Prepare your questions

    Bring 3–5 questions about the team, the role, how we work, and what success looks like in the first 90 days.

  5. 5

    Logistics

    In-person: open your location page below for address, parking, check-in, and NDA instructions.
    Virtual: test camera/mic, find a quiet space, and join 2–3 minutes early via your calendar link.

Interview day

Dress code & what to bring

Dress code

Business casual — wear what makes you comfortable. Most candidates choose slacks or jeans with a collared shirt or blouse. No suit required unless you prefer one.

What to bring (in-person)

A valid government-issued photo ID (required for check-in). Notebook and pen are welcome. Only bring a laptop or portfolio if your recruiter requested it.

Virtual interviews

Laptop or desktop with stable internet, camera, and microphone. Headphones recommended. Have your resume handy as a reference.

Timing

Arrive 10 minutes early in person. For virtual, join 2–3 minutes before your scheduled start. Running late? Email your Recruiting Coordinator right away.
