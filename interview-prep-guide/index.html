Before your interview

1

Review your work

Review responsibilities and required skills listed in the job description. Think of 1–2 real examples from your experience for each major area.
2

Learn about us

Skim the links below — our careers site, product overview, and values. Genuine curiosity matters more than memorizing facts.
3

Prepare questions

Bring 3–5 questions about the team, the role, how we work, and what success looks like in the first 90 days.
4

Get excited

Remember that you're in the running for a reason. We're looking forward to getting to know you more!

Day-of interview FAQ

Will there be a lunch interview?

If your interview loop includes lunch, your Recruiting Coordinator will confirm the details in advance. Please share any dietary restrictions with your coordinator as early as possible.

What do I bring?

Bring a valid government ID (in-person). A notebook and pen are welcome. Only bring a laptop if your recruiter requested a presentation. Please keep your phone on silent during interviews.

I need a reasonable accommodation request

Box is committed to providing reasonable accommodations for candidates. If you need an accommodation for your interview, please contact your Recruiting Coordinator as soon as possible so we can support you.

What if I'm running late?

Contact your Recruiting Coordinator immediately. We'll do our best to adjust, but some sessions may need to be rescheduled.

What do I wear?

Business casual — wear what makes you comfortable. Most candidates choose slacks or jeans with a collared shirt or blouse. No suit required unless you prefer one.
