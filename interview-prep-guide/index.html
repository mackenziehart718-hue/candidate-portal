Before your interview

1

Review your work

Review responsibilities and required skills listed in the job description. Think of 1–2 real examples from your experience for each major area.
2

Learn about us

Skim the links below — our careers site, product overview, and values. Genuine curiosity matters more than memorizing facts.
3

Prepare questions

Bring 3–5 questions about the team, the role, how we work, and what success looks like in the first 90 days.
4

Get excited

Remember that you're in the running for a reason. We're looking forward to getting to know you more!

Day-of interview FAQ

Will there be a lunch during my interview?

If your interview is during lunch hours, we will include a break for you to have lunch with someone from the team. The lunch break will not be treated as an interview, so please enjoy it.

What do I bring?

Please bring a valid government ID (in-person) to verify your identity at check-in. Please bring a laptop if you are completing a technical assessment or presentation. If you need to borrow one, please let your Recruiting partners know. Some candidates may choose to bring a notebook and pen but this is optional.

I need a reasonable accommodation request

Box is committed to providing reasonable accommodations for candidates. If you need an accommodation for your interview, please contact your Recruiting Coordinator as soon as possible so we can support you.

What if I'm running late?

Please contact your Recruiting Coordinator immediately. We'll do our best to adjust, but some sessions may need to be rescheduled.

What do I wear?

Business casual — wear what makes you comfortable. Most candidates choose slacks or jeans with a collared shirt or blouse. No suit required unless you prefer one.

Do I need to sign a Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) before my interview?

Before your scheduled interview, please ensure that you electronically sign our NDA using Box Sign through the link provided by your Recruiting Coordinator. All candidates are required to sign in order to begin their interview. If you have any questions regarding the NDA form, please reach out to your Recruiting Coordinator.
